## Data storage and backups

OsteoMap has no server and no user accounts. It never sends your records over the internet, and nobody else keeps a copy of them.

**Where records are kept.** Records are saved in the web browser's own storage (IndexedDB) on the device you are using, for the web address OsteoMap was opened from. They are not synchronised anywhere:

- Each device, each browser and each browser profile has its own separate set of records.
- On iPhone and iPad, OsteoMap installed on the Home Screen keeps its records separately from OsteoMap opened in Safari.
- A copy of OsteoMap at a different web address (for example, after it moves to a new host or domain) starts empty. Records at the old address stay there.

**How records can be lost.** Records can be deleted, without warning from OsteoMap, when:

- browsing data, site data or cookies are cleared, by you, by a cleaning tool, or by an organisation's device policy;
- OsteoMap is used in a private or incognito window, which deletes its records when the window closes;
- the browser or the installed app is removed, or the device is reset, replaced, lost or damaged;
- the browser deletes stored data to free up space, or because the site has not been used for a while (some browsers, including Safari, do this).

OsteoMap asks the browser to keep its storage permanently, but browsers may refuse.

**Keeping backups.** OsteoMap does not make backups automatically. Back up your records yourself:

1. Open **Import / Export** and choose **Export Full Backup (.csv)**.
2. Store the file somewhere other than the device, such as your organisation's file storage.
3. Do this regularly, and always before clearing browser data, changing or resetting a device, or moving to a copy of OsteoMap at a different web address.
4. Now and then, check that a backup opens and contains the records you expect.

To restore records, or to move them to another device or address, use **Import / Export** to import the backup file.

**Security.** OsteoMap does not encrypt records or exported files. Anyone who can use the device and browser profile, or who has an exported file, can read them. Protect devices and files to suit how sensitive your records are.
